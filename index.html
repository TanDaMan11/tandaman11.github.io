SWARM

Kill monsters and buy upgrades

PLAY

Game Overview

Swarm is an action-packed survival game where you face waves of monsters. Defeat enemies, collect drops, and purchase powerful upgrades to become stronger and survive longer waves.

Controls

  • Movement: Arrow Keys or WASD
  • Attack: Mouse Click or Space Bar
  • Buy Upgrades: Press 'U' or click the upgrade menu

Strategies & Tips

  • Early Game: Focus on basic damage upgrades before defense
  • Wave Management: Keep moving to avoid being surrounded
  • Upgrade Priority: Balance damage and health upgrades for sustainability
  • Resource Management: Save money for critical mid-game upgrades
  • Positioning: Stay near the center of the map to have more escape routes

Advanced Tactics

Once you unlock special abilities, combine them with movement patterns to create deadly combinations. Always prioritize mobility upgrades in later waves to kite enemies effectively.

Be a stock investor

PLAY

Game Overview

Trade Sim is a realistic stock market simulation game. Buy and sell virtual stocks, analyze market trends, and build your investment portfolio to maximize profits. Learn real trading principles while having fun!

How to Play

  • Start with a beginning capital amount
  • Research stock performance and market trends
  • Buy undervalued stocks and sell at peak prices

  • Monitor your total net worth and portfolio performance

Trading Strategies

  • Day Trading: Buy and sell the same day for quick profits
  • Long-term Investing: Hold stocks for consistent growth
  • Portfolio Diversification: Spread investments across different sectors
  • Trend Following: Identify and trade with market momentum
  • Risk Management: Never put all capital into one stock

Beginner Tips

Start small to understand market mechanics. Don't panic during market downturns. Research stocks before investing. Keep a cash reserve for opportunities.

1V1 FIGHT

Fight your friends

PLAY

Game Overview

1V1 Fight is a competitive combat game where you duel against an opponent. Choose your fighter, master different combat techniques, and defeat your adversary in intense one-on-one battles.

Basic Controls

  • Movement: Arrow Keys or WASD
  • Light Attack: Left Mouse Button
  • Heavy Attack: Right Mouse Button
  • Block: Space Bar
  • Special Move: Q or E Key

Combat Strategies

  • Spacing: Maintain proper distance to land hits while avoiding damage
  • Timing: Perfect your attack timing to interrupt opponent moves
  • Combos: Chain attacks together for massive damage
  • Defense: Block heavy attacks to reduce damage taken
  • Special Moves: Use special abilities strategically when you have the advantage

Pro Tips

Don't spam attacks - practice patience and wait for openings. Learn your character's strengths and weaknesses. Study opponent patterns to predict their moves. Use the environment to your advantage.

PIXEL DODGE

Dodge falling objects

PLAY

Game Overview

Pixel Dodge is a fast-paced reflex game where objects fall from the sky. Navigate your pixel character to avoid collisions while collecting power-ups. How long can you survive?

Controls

  • Move Left: Left Arrow or A Key
  • Move Right: Right Arrow or D Key
  • Quick Dash: Space Bar (limited uses)

Game Tips & Strategies

  • Anticipate Movement: Predict where objects will land
  • Center Positioning: Stay in the middle to have maximum escape routes
  • Power-up Priority: Collect invincibility shields over speed boosts early
  • Reaction Time: Focus and keep your reflexes sharp
  • Pattern Recognition: Objects follow predictable patterns - learn them!

Difficulty Levels

Game difficulty increases as your score grows. Objects fall faster, spawn more frequently, and new obstacle types appear. Achieve high scores by mastering the fundamentals and staying calm under pressure.

Duel

A fast rhythm game

PLAY

Game Overview

Duel is an electrifying rhythm game that tests your timing and coordination. Press buttons in sync with the music and hit the notes accurately to build massive combo chains and achieve high scores.

How to Play

  • Watch the notes approach the hit zone
  • Press the corresponding key when notes reach the center
  • Nail the timing for "Perfect" scores
  • Build combo chains for multiplier bonuses
  • Keep up with increasingly faster songs

Key Bindings

  • Left Lane: A Key
  • Center Lane: S Key
  • Right Lane: D Key
  • Special Note: Space Bar

Mastering the Game

  • Timing is Everything: Precision beats speed
  • Listen to the Beat: Sync with the music rhythm
  • Combo Chains: Consecutive hits multiply your score
  • Practice Harder Songs: Unlock better rewards with higher difficulties
  • Warm Up: Start with easier songs to get in rhythm

Advanced Tips

Learn song patterns through multiple playthroughs. Adjust hit timing sensitivity if needed. Stay relaxed - tension affects accuracy. Aim for "Perfect" hits on every note to maximize your final score and leaderboard ranking.
